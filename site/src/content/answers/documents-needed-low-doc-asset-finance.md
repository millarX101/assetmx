## Direct answer
A low-doc asset finance application on AssetMX needs four things: your ABN and GST status (checked automatically), director photo ID, recent business bank statements, and the asset invoice or quote. Full financials such as tax returns or BAS are not required for applications within lender low-doc policy.

## The document checklist

| Document | Purpose | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| ABN/GST lookup | Confirms business registration and GST status | Automated, no upload needed |
| Director ID | Verifies identity of the guarantor(s) | Driver licence is standard |
| Business bank statements | Bank-statement verification of trading activity | 3 to 6 months |
| Asset invoice or quote | Confirms what is being financed and its price | Invoice needed by settlement; a quote is enough for conditional approval |

## Why full financials are not required
Low-doc asset finance is built for ABN businesses that fit a lender's low-doc policy: an ABN active for 24 or more months, GST registered, at least one director or guarantor, and business use of the asset. Because the application is verified through bank statements and automated ABN/GST checks rather than full financial statements, tax returns and BAS are not needed for a standard application. A lender may still request them if the deal is larger or more complex, or falls outside standard policy. See [low-doc asset finance requirements](/answers/low-doc-asset-finance-requirements) for the full eligibility criteria.

## Private sale documents
If you are buying from a private seller rather than a dealer, you will also need a contract of sale or similar written agreement instead of a dealer invoice, and the asset may need an inspection. See [private sale asset finance](/answers/private-sale-asset-finance) for details.
