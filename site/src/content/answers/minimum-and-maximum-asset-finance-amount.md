## Direct answer
AssetMX arranges asset finance from $5,000 up to $500,000, over terms of 12 to 84 months. This range applies to ABN businesses trading for 24 or more months, registered for GST, that fit lender low-doc policy.

## The amount and term range

| Feature | Range |
|---|---|
| Minimum loan amount | $5,000 |
| Maximum loan amount | $500,000 |
| Minimum term | 12 months |
| Maximum term | 84 months |

Within this range, indicative rates vary by term: 8.49% for 12 months, 7.49% for 24 months, and 6.89% for 36 to 60 months (indicative, verified 16 February 2026). See [asset finance interest rates in Australia](/answers/asset-finance-interest-rates-australia) for the full rate detail.

## What determines how much you can borrow
The amount available depends on the asset being financed, its value, and your business's eligibility. The standard low-doc criteria apply regardless of loan size:

- ABN active for 24 or more months
- GST registered
- At least one director or guarantor
- Asset no older than 15 years at end of term
- Business use of the asset

See [low-doc asset finance requirements](/answers/low-doc-asset-finance-requirements) for the full detail.

## Balloon payments and loan size
For longer terms, a balloon or residual payment can reduce the amount repaid during the term, calculated on the asset value rather than the amount financed. Maximum balloon amounts are capped by term: 65% for 1 year, 60% for 2 years, 50% for 3 years, 40% for 4 years, and 30% for 5 years or more. See [what is a balloon payment](/answers/what-is-a-balloon-payment) for how this affects your repayments.
