## Direct answer
Asset finance approval on AssetMX moves in three stages: an online quote, conditional approval, and settlement. Getting a quote takes about 60 seconds. If your application fits lender low-doc policy, conditional approval is typically given the same business day. Settlement then follows once the asset invoice and signed loan documents are received, often within 24-48 hours.

## The approval timeline
A low-doc application that meets policy moves quickly because it relies on automated checks rather than manual review of full financials.

| Stage | What happens | Typical timing |
|---|---|---|
| Quote | Online form, indicative rate and repayment shown | About 60 seconds |
| Conditional approval | Lender reviews ABN/GST status, director ID and bank statements | Same business day (in-policy) |
| Settlement | Asset invoice and signed documents processed, funds released | Often 24-48 hours |

These timeframes apply to applications that fit lender low-doc policy: an ABN active for 24 or more months, GST registered, at least one director or guarantor, and business use of the asset. A deal that falls outside policy, or needs full financials, will take longer because a lender may request tax returns or BAS before approving.

## What speeds up settlement
The main thing that determines how fast you settle is how quickly your documents are ready. Having your director ID, 3 to 6 months of business bank statements, and the signed asset invoice or quote on hand before you apply removes the usual back-and-forth. ## Credit checks and your file
A quote on AssetMX does not touch your credit file. A credit check on the business and its directors only happens once you submit a formal application. For more detail see [does asset finance affect my credit score](/answers/does-asset-finance-affect-my-credit-score).
