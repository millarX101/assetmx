## Direct answer
Yes. Low-doc asset finance is designed specifically for equipment finance without tax returns or full financial statements. An ABN business trading for 24 months or more, registered for GST, with at least one director or guarantor, can typically borrow between $5,000 and $500,000 for business equipment, provided the application fits the lender's low-doc policy.

## What is checked instead of financials
Instead of tax returns and full financials, a low-doc application is assessed using:

| Item | Purpose |
|---|---|
| ABN/GST lookup (automated) | Confirms trading history and GST registration |
| Director ID (driver licence) | Identity verification |
| 3-6 months business bank statements | Verifies cash flow |
| Asset invoice or quote | Matches the loan to the equipment being financed |

## When financials might still be requested
Low-doc policy covers most standard equipment purchases within the $5,000 to $500,000 range. For a larger or more complex deal, a lender may still ask for further documents, such as financials, at its own discretion, even though this is not required for a standard in-policy low-doc application.

## Why this works for established businesses
A 24-month trading history and GST registration give a lender enough confidence in the business without needing to review its full financial history, which is why the process can move quickly for equipment purchases that fit within policy.
