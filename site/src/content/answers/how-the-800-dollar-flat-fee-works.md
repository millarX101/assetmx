## Direct answer

AssetMX's fee model is a single $800 flat fee for arranging the finance, charged once per deal rather than as a percentage loaded into the interest rate. It sits alongside the lender's base rate as its own line item, so a reader can see exactly what is going to the platform and what is going to the lender. It can be paid upfront at settlement or financed into the loan amount.

## What the fee covers and what it does not

The $800 fee covers matching your application to lender low-doc policy, preparing the application and coordinating settlement. It does not include the lender's own costs. Most lenders also charge a typical $500 establishment fee, which is usually financed into the loan rather than paid upfront, though the exact amount varies by lender. If a private-sale asset needs an inspection, a separate $250 inspection fee applies.

## How this compares with commission-based pricing

Many brokers are paid through commission loaded into the interest rate, typically 1-2%, which is worth roughly $3,000 to $6,000 in extra interest on a $100,000 deal, and some charge an origination fee on top. Because that cost is built into the rate rather than shown as a fee, it can be harder to see. AssetMX's flat fee is fixed regardless of loan size, so it does not scale up on larger deals the way a percentage-based commission does.
