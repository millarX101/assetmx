## Direct answer

Requesting a quote on AssetMX is an information step, not a credit application, so it has no impact on your credit file. A credit check only occurs when you move to a formal application and a lender assesses it against its low-doc policy, at which point the business and its directors or guarantors are checked as part of standard lending practice.

## How the process separates quoting from applying

The online quote takes about 60 seconds and is built from the numbers you enter and standard eligibility criteria such as ABN age, GST registration and loan term. Because no credit file is accessed at this stage, there is no reason to hesitate before getting an indicative figure. It is only when you decide to proceed and submit a formal application that the lender runs its credit assessment. For an application that fits low-doc policy, conditional approval is typically returned the same business day.

## Who gets checked and why

Lenders check the credit history of the business itself and of at least one director or guarantor, since low-doc policy requires at least one guarantor on the loan. This is standard practice across asset finance and is not specific to AssetMX. The check happens once, at the formal application stage, rather than repeatedly as you compare terms or amounts on a quote.

## What happens after approval

| Stage | What happens | Credit file impact |
|---|---|---|
| Online quote | Indicative numbers based on your inputs | None |
| Formal application | Lender assesses business and director credit | Credit check recorded |
| Settlement | Invoice and signed documents processed, often 24-48 hours | None additional |
