## Direct answer
Buying a vehicle or piece of equipment from a private seller does not rule out asset finance. AssetMX can finance a private-sale asset provided it meets the usual eligibility rules on age and business use. The main differences from a dealer purchase are the documents used and, in some cases, an inspection.

## What is different about a private sale
Because there is no dealer invoice or dealer warranty behind a private sale, two things commonly apply:

| Item | Dealer purchase | Private sale |
|---|---|---|
| Purchase document | Dealer invoice | Contract of sale |
| Inspection | Not usually required | May be required, $250 fee |
| Eligibility | Standard low-doc criteria | Same standard low-doc criteria |

The $250 inspection fee applies when a private-sale asset needs an inspection to confirm its condition and value before the lender will finance it. Not every private-sale deal requires one.

## Eligibility is unchanged
The same rules apply whether the asset comes from a dealer or a private seller:

- ABN active for 24 or more months
- GST registered
- At least one director or guarantor
- Asset no older than 15 years at end of term
- Business use of the asset

See [low-doc asset finance requirements](/answers/low-doc-asset-finance-requirements) for full detail, and [financing used vehicles and equipment](/answers/financing-used-vehicles-and-equipment) for how the age limit is calculated.

## Documents you will need
In place of a dealer invoice, you will need a signed contract of sale showing the asset details, the seller's details and the agreed price. The rest of the document checklist is the same as any low-doc application: ABN/GST lookup, director ID, and recent business bank statements. See [documents needed for low-doc asset finance](/answers/documents-needed-low-doc-asset-finance).
