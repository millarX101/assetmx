## Direct answer
Used vehicles and equipment are financed on AssetMX the same way as new ones, from $5,000 to $500,000, as long as the asset will be no older than 15 years at the end of the finance term and is used for business purposes. This makes low-doc finance available for a wide range of second-hand trucks, utes, trailers and equipment, not just new purchases.

## The age rule explained
The 15-year limit is measured at the end of the term, not at settlement. A 10-year-old truck financed over a 3-year term will be 13 years old at the end of the loan, which fits within policy. The same truck financed over a 7-year term would be 17 years old at the end, which does not fit. Choosing a shorter term is often the way to make an older asset work within lender policy.

## Eligibility is the same as for new assets
Whether the asset is new or used, the standard low-doc criteria apply:

- ABN active for 24 or more months
- GST registered
- At least one director or guarantor
- Asset no older than 15 years at end of term
- Business use of the asset

See [low-doc asset finance requirements](/answers/low-doc-asset-finance-requirements) for the full detail.

## Dealer vs private sale
Buying a used vehicle or piece of equipment from a licensed dealer is the most straightforward path, using a standard dealer invoice. Buying from a private seller is also possible, but the asset may need an inspection, which carries a $250 fee, and you will need a contract of sale rather than a dealer invoice. See [private sale asset finance](/answers/private-sale-asset-finance) for what to expect.

## Structuring the finance
Used vehicles and equipment can typically be financed under a chattel mortgage, where your business owns the asset from settlement. Compare this against a finance lease at [chattel mortgage vs finance lease](/answers/chattel-mortgage-vs-finance-lease).
