## Direct answer
Low-doc asset finance has a fixed set of eligibility rules set by lender policy, not by AssetMX. Your business needs an ABN that has been active for at least 24 months, current GST registration, and at least one director or guarantor standing behind the loan. The asset itself needs to be used for business purposes and must not be older than 15 years by the time the loan term ends.

## The hard requirements
| Requirement | Standard |
|---|---|
| ABN age | 24+ months active |
| GST registration | Required |
| Guarantor | At least one director or guarantor |
| Asset age | No older than 15 years at end of term |
| Asset use | Business use |

These are hard requirements. An application outside any one of them will not fit standard low-doc policy and would need to be assessed differently, if at all.

## The soft consideration
Business use under 50% is not an automatic decline, but it can affect which rates and lenders are available, since low-doc pricing is built around predominantly business-use assets.

## Why these rules exist
Lenders use trading history and GST registration as a proxy for business stability instead of requesting full financials. A 24-month track record and GST registration give the lender enough confidence to skip tax returns and full financial statements for an in-policy deal. This keeps the process fast for businesses that qualify, while still managing lender risk.
