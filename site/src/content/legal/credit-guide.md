## 2. Our Credit Services

We provide credit assistance by:

- Helping you assess your business asset finance needs
- Providing indicative quotes and comparisons
- Collecting and processing your application information
- Submitting applications to our panel of lenders on your behalf
- Assisting with the settlement process

We are a credit licensee, which means we hold our own Australian Credit Licence and are directly responsible for our credit activities.

## 3. The Lenders We Work With

We have relationships with a panel of lenders who provide asset finance products. Our lending panel includes banks, non-bank lenders, and specialist asset financiers.

When you submit an application, we will assess which lenders from our panel may be suitable for your needs based on your circumstances, credit profile, and the asset being financed.

We do not provide advice on which lender or product is best for you. We present options based on likely approval and your stated preferences.

## 4. How We Are Paid

### 4.1 Our Platform Fee

We charge a flat platform fee of **$800 (inclusive of GST)** for our services. This fee is:

- Only payable if your finance application settles
- Disclosed separately from the lender's interest rate
- Added to your loan amount at settlement (not paid upfront)

### 4.2 Other Payments We May Receive

We do not receive commissions from lenders. Our only fee is the $800 platform fee disclosed above.

From time to time, we may receive:

- Referral fees from partner businesses
- Bonuses from dealers or equipment suppliers

If any such payments apply to your transaction, we will fully disclose them to you before you accept a finance offer.

### 4.3 Example

For a $100,000 equipment loan:

- Platform fee: $800
- That's it - no hidden commissions or trail payments

Our flat fee model means we're incentivised to get you the best deal, not to steer you toward lenders who pay us more.

## 6. Credit Checks and Your Credit File

When you submit an application, we will request a credit report from one or more credit reporting bodies (Equifax, illion, or Experian). This will be recorded on your credit file as a "credit enquiry".

Multiple credit enquiries in a short period may affect your credit score. We recommend only submitting an application when you are ready to proceed.

Using our online calculator does not affect your credit score. Credit checks are only performed when you submit a full application.
